Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about our products, pricing, or delivery? Here are some helpful answers.

Do you keep all products in stock?

Products may be sourced to order through our suppliers. Contact us to confirm availability before placing an order.

How do I get a price quote?

Submit our Request a Quote form, call us, or contact us on WhatsApp. Share the product name and quantity so we can check availability and provide pricing.

Can you find a product that is not listed on your website?

Yes, we can check with our suppliers for other printer supplies, replacement parts, and office essentials. Availability depends on the product and supplier.

Do you serve individuals as well as businesses?

Yes. Individuals, home-office customers, and businesses can contact us to request product availability and pricing.

Do you offer delivery in Houston?

Contact us with your location and order details to confirm local delivery options, availability, and timing.

Can you ship orders outside Houston?

Shipping to other areas of the United States may be available depending on the product and order. Contact us to confirm shipping options and costs.
